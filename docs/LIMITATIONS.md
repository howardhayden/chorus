# Limitations

## Purpose

This document owns limitations inherent to CHORUS as a model and browser
product. Expected recovery from a specific bad input or boundary condition
belongs to [Edge cases and failure behavior](EDGE-CASES.md). Checks not yet run
for a particular build belong to [Release status](RELEASE-STATUS.md) and its
machine-readable evidence record.

## Modeling limits

- CHORUS is an explanatory fictional system, not an empirical model of a
  named community, institution, platform, profession, political group, class,
  region, family, or individual.
- Authored interior states make motive playable for a fictional seat. They do
  not make motive reliably inferable from real speech, warmth, reserve,
  vocabulary, status, register, relationship, or audience response.
- Coefficients express designed causal pressure inside one simulation. They
  are not measured effect sizes, population estimates, probabilities, or
  predictions.
- The generated communication repertoire is deliberately legible. It cannot
  represent the full historical, multilingual, embodied, or situational range
  of human linguistic practice.
- The relationship plot shows authored outward ties and statuses. Missing
  edges do not assert that no real relationship exists, and visible edges do
  not establish trust, agreement, motive, or truth.
- Fatigue channels separate discernment from follow-through for the designed
  system. They are not clinical constructs or an assessment of a player.
- Conflict, escalation, repair, internal review, and strategic behavior appear
  only when the scenario grammar finds a compatible situation. Their absence
  is not a judgment that a real situation requires no such lens.

## Narrative and content limits

- A night contains six rooms and four beats per room. Generation is uncapped
  across nights, but each night uses that fixed geometry.
- Incidents are PG, low-stakes fictional composites. The system is not a
  rehearsal for violence, emergency response, legal process, clinical care,
  abuse assessment, or high-risk workplace intervention.
- Deliberate misrepresentation and diversion are represented at a descriptive
  social-system level. The game intentionally omits operational instructions
  for manipulation, targeting, harassment, or evasion.
- A deterministic grammar can produce substantial variation without the open
  semantic range of authored long-form fiction. Coherence gates favor bounded
  validity over unrestricted novelty.
- English is the current interface and scenario language. Register variation
  is represented through readable discourse choices, not accent imitation or
  exhaustive regional vocabulary.

## Interaction and accessibility limits

- The application targets WCAG 2.0 AA as a minimum and adds stronger reflow,
  focus, motion, and disclosure contracts. Automated checks cannot certify an
  entire conformance claim or every browser/assistive-technology combination.
- The visual relationship plot has a filterable text equivalent because node
  placement alone cannot communicate every relationship reliably. The plot is
  an exploration aid, not the sole evidence surface.
- The one-viewport shell intentionally uses named internal scroll regions.
  Long-form notebook and release evidence pages use ordinary document
  scrolling instead.
- Film and motion preferences affect presentation only. They do not provide a
  substitute design for browsers that cannot execute the application.
- Very old browsers without required platform features are outside the
  supported contract. The exact verified matrix is release-specific.

## Persistence and privacy limits

- Local slots remain in the current browser profile and can be removed by the
  browser, private-browsing policy, storage pressure, profile reset, or device
  loss.
- Portable text is readable by anyone who obtains the file. It is integrity
  checked, not encrypted, access-controlled, or synchronized.
- Saves are bound to generator and schema compatibility. Unsupported versions
  fail closed; migration is provided only when conversion can be deterministic
  and lossless.
- Session-only play prevents intentional application persistence but cannot
  control browser history, screenshots, operating-system backups, downloaded
  files, extensions, or device-level observation.

## Operational limits

- The playable application is client-local after assets load, but initial
  delivery still depends on whichever static or application host the operator
  selects.
- The repository does not include an account system, remote state service,
  collaborative multiplayer, moderation queue, scenario editor, or live
  content feed.
- Dependency and vulnerability status is time-bound. A clean audit in one
  evidence record does not guarantee future registry or advisory state.
- Executed notebooks declare and calculate fixed technical contracts; they do
  not run the browser application or replace the TypeScript simulation and
  end-to-end suites.

## Non-goals

CHORUS does not rank players, diagnose actors, detect deception, recommend
punishment, predict escalation, infer protected traits, settle a real dispute,
or assign a universal value to a social class, profession, political language,
family role, region, or community.
