# ADR 0004: Conclusion-gated progressive disclosure

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-18

## Context

Naming scapegoating, protection, code mismatch, fatigue, or pressure phases
before play would turn the experience into answer matching and overload the
active decision surface.

## Decision

Expose records, uncertainty, situated pressure, reply conditions, and concrete
actions during play. Seal analytic classifications, complete actor repertoires,
internal structures, and whole-night causal interpretation until all 24
decisions are complete.

## Consequences

- Hidden information must also be absent from accessible markup.
- Guides can point toward evidence habits but cannot reveal the expected label.
- Room-close receipts remain bounded and provisional.
- The debrief requires separate sections instead of one dense report.
- Empty future content is omitted rather than explained by placeholders.

## Alternatives considered

- Up-front lesson objectives were rejected because they pre-solved the game.
- Permanent dashboards were rejected because they overwhelmed the active beat.
- Visually hidden but mounted analysis was rejected because it leaks through
  assistive technology and source inspection.
