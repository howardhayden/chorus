# ADR 0007: Separate discernment and enactment

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-18

## Context

Prosocial protagonists under prolonged platform load may recognize a better
action while losing the follow-through needed to carry it. Treating fatigue as
ignorance erases that distinction; letting fatigue force amplification turns
the model coercive.

## Decision

Model discernment and enactment separately. Five typed fatigue channels may
reduce enactment but never discernment. Keep the better action visible when
blocked and explain the local capacity barrier. Preserve one non-amplification
floor at every beat regardless of enactment depletion.

## Consequences

- A blocked ideal can be understood without being selectable.
- Capacity explanation does not excuse a harmful alternative.
- Reading time and assistive technology cannot add fatigue.
- An extraordinary last resort can appear only under complete high-discernment,
  low-enactment, and extreme-load eligibility and must record split costs.
- Every beat needs a carryable refusal, bound, check, exit, or correction floor.

## Alternatives considered

- One judgment score was rejected because it collapses recognition and action.
- Fatigue-driven random action was rejected because it removes agency and
  responsibility.
- Fully locking every prosocial path was rejected because exhaustion should not
  force repetition, personalization, or amplification.
