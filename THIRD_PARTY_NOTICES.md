# Third-party notices

Third-party packages and tools retain their own copyright and license terms.
The exact direct and transitive dependency graph, registry integrity values, and
package-reported license metadata are recorded in `package-lock.json`; the
source-bound dependency inventory and audit evidence are retained under
`evidence/releases/` and `evidence/runs/`.

Production code directly depends on Next.js, React, and React DOM. Build, test,
lint, documentation, and Cloudflare deployment tools are development
dependencies and remain part of the build and distribution review boundary.
No proprietary notice in this repository overrides a dependency's own terms.

The standard license texts under `LICENSES/` include historical Owner grants.
They are retained as evidence of prior scope and do not replace third-party
package notices or create a new grant over later Owner-controlled material.
